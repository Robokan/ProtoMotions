Official Unitree Go2 description, stripped to the ProtoMotions 17-body skeleton.

Upstream: https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros
  robots/go2_description/urdf/go2_description.urdf
The upstream package does not ship a top-level LICENSE file in the sparse
checkout; Unitree's robot descriptions are typically BSD-3-Clause.

`data/scripts/vendor_official_urdfs.py` renames `base` → `base_link` (the
name used by mjcf/go2_nomesh.xml and the motion clips) and drops rotors,
head collision links, imu/radar/camera. Joint names (FL_hip_joint, …)
already matched the MJCF.
